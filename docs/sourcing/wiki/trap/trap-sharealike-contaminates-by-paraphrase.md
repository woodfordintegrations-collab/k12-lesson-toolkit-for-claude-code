# ShareAlike contaminates a file by close paraphrase

## Summary

Rewriting a task in your own words does not escape ShareAlike. A close paraphrase that follows a
specific task's structure, its numbers and its pedagogical move is an adaptation in copyright
terms however different the wording, and adaptations of a ShareAlike source have to go out under a
ShareAlike licence. On every SA host in this corpus that licence carries NonCommercial as well, so
the obligation that arrives is not the one you started with.

The trap is silent in both directions and this page has to hold both:

- **Under-restricting.** "I reworded it, so it is mine." The wording is not the test. The
  structure, the numbers and the move are.
- **Over-restricting.** "This host is ShareAlike, so I cannot use it." Citation is untouched by
  ShareAlike. Quotation does not trigger it either. This project made exactly that error once and
  its own record calls it, verbatim, "the precise error that caused the earlier sweep to write IM
  off, and it cost us the single most useful source in the field."

the project's governing ruling settles the operating posture and is stricter than any licence here: **the repo
ships CC BY 4.0 and takes no paraphrase from any ShareAlike source, ever.** That ruling removes the
judgment call from the build. This page exists so the reason is understood, so the boundary is
recognisable in material already in hand, and so the ruling is not over-applied to citation and
quotation, which it does not touch.

## When to reach for it

Reach for it the moment a draft sentence is being written *while looking at* a specific source
task, activity, rubric line or worked example, rather than from the standard's own text. That is
the moment the seam is crossed or not crossed, and it is invisible afterwards.

Reach for it before publication of anything assembled from mixed sources. The obligation attaches
on Sharing, not on drafting, so the decision point is later than people assume and the check is
cheap up to that point.

Reach for it when a source's licence string contains `sa`. In this corpus that is the IM 2016 task
bank, EngageNY and its NYSED successor, the MARS PD Modules, the IM-sourced PDFs served by Achieve
the Core, and 6,214 records inside a nominally CC BY 4.0 Learning Commons export. The per-host
inventory of who carries SA at which version belongs to [[license-sharealike]], not here.

Do **not** reach for this page for NoDerivatives, which forbids the operation outright rather than
conditioning it and which no relicensing cures; that is [[license-noderivatives]]. Do not reach for
it to decide whether you may quote: that is [[concept-cite-quote-adapt]], and the answer is usually
yes.

## How it works

**The deed's condition**, verbatim from the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 deed fetched 2026-08-08:

> If you remix, transform, or build upon the material, you must distribute your contributions under the same license as the original.

**The legal code is narrower and more precise**, and the difference is load-bearing for a
repository that drafts before it decides what to publish. Section 3(b), complete, verbatim:

> **ShareAlike.**
>
> In addition to the conditions in Section 3(a), if You Share Adapted Material You produce, the following conditions also apply.
>
> The Adapter’s License You apply must be a Creative Commons license with the same License Elements, this version or later, or a BY-NC-SA Compatible License.
>
> You must include the text of, or the URI or hyperlink to, the Adapter's License You apply. You may satisfy this condition in any reasonable manner based on the medium, means, and context in which You Share Adapted Material.
>
> You may not offer or impose any additional or different terms or conditions on, or apply any Effective Technological Measures to, Adapted Material that restrict exercise of the rights granted under the Adapter's License You apply.

The trigger is `if You Share Adapted Material You produce`. Producing an adaptation that is never
shared triggers nothing. `Share` is itself defined by provision to the public, verbatim: "to provide
material to the public by any means or process that requires permission under the Licensed Rights".

**The seam is in the definition of Adapted Material**, verbatim:

> **Adapted Material** means material subject to Copyright and Similar Rights that is derived from or based upon the Licensed Material and in which the Licensed Material is translated, altered, arranged, transformed, or otherwise modified in a manner requiring permission under the Copyright and Similar Rights held by the Licensor.

The operative phrase is `modified in a manner requiring permission`. Where no permission would have
been required in the first place, no Adapted Material exists and Section 3(b) never fires. That is
why writing original expression from a standard's own statement text is clean: the standard text is
not the SA source's expression at all.

**One further trigger a curriculum repo can hit without rewriting anything.** Section 4(b),
verbatim, makes a database Adapted Material for Section 3(b) purposes where "You include all or a
substantial portion of the database contents in a database in which You have Sui Generis Database
Rights". Bulk-ingesting an SA-licensed collection into a queryable store can therefore fire the
clause even though no individual item was touched.

## In practice

**The working test.** Ask three questions of the draft sentence, in this order. Any yes moves it
across the seam.

1. **Structure.** Does it follow the source item's sequence of moves: same setup, same intermediate
   step, same reveal?
2. **Numbers.** Does it carry the source's specific quantities, dimensions, coordinates or answer,
   where a different set would have served the same standard equally well?
3. **Pedagogical move.** Is the thing that makes it work as teaching, the chosen representation, the
   deliberate misconception it baits, the order the constraint is relaxed, the source's choice
   rather than the standard's?

Those three criteria are this project's own adjudication of the seam, recorded in its twelve-host
verdict document, not a formulation published by Creative Commons.

**The two clean routes, and the one that is not clean.** Recorded verbatim in this project's
evidence file on the IM task bank:

> Citing is not redistributing and is unaffected. But paraphrase-and-republish at the level of an adaptation triggers SA: the derivative must ship under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (or compatible), which would license the whole repo NC. Original expression written from the standard, with IM cited as inspiration, avoids this. Close paraphrase of a specific task does not.

So: write from the standard text and cite the SA source as inspiration, or quote it and attribute
it. Do not rewrite it.

**Quotation is not adaptation.** The twelve-host verdict document states the reason, verbatim:
"because a quotation set inside your own original prose with attribution is not an adaptation of the
quoted work, it is a use of it." A quoted, attributed passage sitting inside your own writing leaves
your writing under your own licence.

**What the SA host actually says, so the trigger is recognisable in the wild.** The IM 2016 task
bank footer, byte-matched on all 24 in-scope HSG-SRT task pages with zero per-task overrides:

> Typeset May 4, 2016 at 18:58:52. Licensed by Illustrative Mathematics under a
> Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License.

That host and `im.kendallhunt.com` carry near-identical lesson titles under two different grants.
Resolve the host from the URL before any verdict attaches; see [[source-im-task-bank]] and
[[source-im-kendall-hunt]].

## Gotchas & constraints

**1. "Contaminates a file" is the practical effect, not the licence's own wording, and the
difference matters.** The deed says the obligation attaches to `your contributions`, not to the
whole work you place them in. This project's IM evidence file reads the consequence as licensing
"the whole repo NC", and that is that report's reading rather than a quotation from the licence.
Whether a surrounding file becomes Adapted Material or remains a mere collection is not resolved by
anything staged in this wiki. Both readings point the same way in practice: keep SA-derived material
out of files you intend to ship under CC BY 4.0. Quarantine it or do not create it.

**2. The version you must apply is wider than "the same".** The legal code permits a Creative
Commons licence with the same License Elements, `this version or later, or a BY-NC-SA Compatible
License`. The deed's word `same` is looser than the licence, in the licensee's favour.

**3. This wiki cannot tell you which licences are BY-NC-SA compatible.**
`https://creativecommons.org/compatiblelicenses` was not fetched by the staging agent, and the
staged extract says so explicitly. Any claim that a specific licence is compatible is unverified
from here. What would close it: fetch that page and record its list with a date.

**4. The version is genuinely ambiguous on three of the SA sources, and 3.0 is not 4.0.** NYSED's
live statement names no version and links 3.0 Unported while every per-resource EngageNY badge links
3.0 US, a different legal instrument. The MARS PD Modules page shows no version in its visible text
and only the href says 3.0. One Achieve the Core PDF states 4.0 International on one page and 3.0
Unported on another. Under 3.0 and earlier the indication-of-changes duty applies only when you
create a derivative; under 4.0 it applies whenever you Share. Do not silently upgrade a 3.0 label.

**5. NonCommercial rides along, and it is permanent in a way ShareAlike is not.** Every SA source in
this corpus is BY-NC-SA, never plain BY-SA. Taking an adaptation therefore ships an NC term into
whatever it lands in. That is dormant while nothing is sold and unlicensable the moment something
is. See [[license-noncommercial]].

**6. A machine-readable licence field is not sufficient evidence that SA is absent.** In the
Learning Commons v1.11.0 export every one of 247,786 nodes carries `license` as
`https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`, while 6,214 records carry an
`attributionStatement` asserting the opposite. The Georgia mathematics variant, verbatim, on 1,699
records:

> Knowledge Graph is provided by Learning Commons under the CC BY-4.0 license. Georgia Mathematics standards are provided by the Georgia Department of Education, which asserts a CC BY-NC-SA license.

Those records are outside this unit's path, on Multi-State and California, but they prove the field
alone is not a check. See [[source-learning-commons-kg]] and [[concept-chain-of-title]].

**7. ShareAlike does not reach citation, and this project has paid for forgetting that.** Naming a
source, linking it, stating which standard it addresses and describing in your own words what it
does needs no licence at all. Writing off a whole organisation because one of its three hosts
carries SA cost this project its most useful source. See [[concept-curate-and-cite]] and
[[practice-cite-without-redistributing]].
