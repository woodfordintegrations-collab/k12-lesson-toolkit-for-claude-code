# NoDerivatives (the ND rider)

## Summary

ND is the rider that surprises people, because they arrive expecting NonCommercial to be the
binding term. **ND forbids paraphrase-and-republish outright, even non-commercially, even by a
school, even with perfect attribution.** It is the reason an excellent, exactly-on-standard
assessment bank in this corpus is cite-only rather than adaptable.

Three things to carry away.

**The word "NoDerivatives" does not appear on the host.** On `map.mathshell.org` the rider is
stated three separate ways in plain English: "as-is", "unmodified", and "without modification". The
instrument itself is named only in the link label, `[Creative Commons Attribution, Non-commercial,
No Derivatives License 3.0]`, and in the `href`.

**The deed and the licence do not agree about what you may make.** The deed says you may modify
but not distribute the result. The 3.0 legal code grants no adaptation right in the first place.
The safe reading is the licence.

**ND does permit one thing people assume it forbids.** Verbatim redistribution of a whole
unmodified file is licensed, non-commercially, with attribution. The rider bites on rewriting, not
on copying.

## When to reach for it

Reach for this page when a notice says a work may be reproduced "as-is", "unmodified" or "without
modification", with or without a Creative Commons name attached. Reach for it when you are about
to write "NC blocks this", because here the NC and ND riders arrive together on the same host and
it is ND that decides the verdict.

Reach for it when you have a genuinely on-standard task bank and want to know why you may still
not rewrite one of its items.

Do not reach for this page to decide that `map.mathshell.org` is ND site-wide. It is not: the PD
Modules carry ShareAlike instead, which is the opposite kind of grant. See [[license-sharealike]].

## How it works

### The deed has two freedoms where every sibling has three

CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 deed, `You are free to`, in full:

> **Share** — copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format

> The licensor cannot revoke these freedoms as long as you follow the license terms.

**There is no `Adapt` freedom.** Measured: this list has exactly two items where every other
licence deed in the cluster has three. The absence is the fact. The condition, verbatim:

> **NoDerivatives** — If you remix, transform, or build upon the material, you may not distribute the modified material.

`remix, transform, or build upon` links to a footnote, verbatim:

> **remix, transform, or build upon** — Merely changing the format never creates a derivative.

### The legal code grants no adaptation right at all

This is the divergence, and it runs the wrong way for a reader who trusts the deed. The deed's
sentence is conditional and speaks only to distribution, implying you may make a modified version
so long as you keep it private. The licence does not grant the right to make one.

Legal code Section 3, complete, verbatim:

> **3. License Grant.**
>
> Subject to the terms and conditions of this License, Licensor hereby grants You a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual (for the duration of the applicable copyright) license to exercise the rights in the Work as stated below:
>
> to Reproduce the Work, to incorporate the Work into one or more Collections, and to Reproduce the Work as incorporated in the Collections;
>
> to Distribute and Publicly Perform the Work including as incorporated in Collections
>
> The above rights may be exercised in all media and formats whether now known or hereafter devised. The above rights include the right to make such modifications as are technically necessary to exercise the rights in other media and formats. Subject to Section 8(e), all rights not expressly granted by Licensor are hereby reserved, including but not limited to the rights set forth in Section 4(d).

Two limbs only: reproduce, and distribute or publicly perform. Measured against its siblings, the
string `to create and Reproduce Adaptations` appears **1 time** in the CC BY 3.0 legal code,
**1 time** in the CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 legal code, and **0 times** here. The closing sentence then
reserves everything not granted. The only modification right is technical: `such modifications as
are technically necessary to exercise the rights in other media and formats`. **That is narrower
than the deed's format footnote sounds**, because it is limited to what a media or format shift
technically requires.

### What follows, and what does not

Because the grant never reaches adaptation, the operative verdict for this wiki is `cite_only`,
and a page must not soften that to "you may adapt privately" on the strength of the deed alone.

Two consequences run the other way and are easy to overstate. **ShareAlike is not present**:
measured, the `Under the following terms` list has exactly four items, `Attribution`,
`NonCommercial`, `NoDerivatives` and `No additional restrictions`. There is no copyleft, because
there is no adaptation to carry one. And **the indication of changes is not required by this
licence**. The deed's Attribution clause demands it, but that is 4.0 template wording served on a
3.0 page; Section 4(c) carries no such requirement, and there is no derivative for it to attach to.

Attribution under 3.0 requires more than 4.0 does: all copyright notices kept intact, plus the
author or designated Attribution Parties, **the title of the Work if supplied**, and the URI.
Measured: `title of the Work` appears 0 times in the CC BY 4.0, CC BY-NC 4.0 and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
legal codes. Section 4(a) separately requires the licence itself to travel, verbatim:

> You may Distribute or Publicly Perform the Work only under the terms of this License. You must include a copy of, or the Uniform Resource Identifier (URI) for, this License with every copy of the Work You Distribute or Publicly Perform.

### One naming quirk that defeats a grep

The served deed uses two spellings. Its `<title>` and heading say **NoDerivs**, while the condition
inside the body is labelled **NoDerivatives**. Both are in the bytes, 2 occurrences each. The 4.0
successor named in the deed's own upgrade banner is spelled `NoDerivatives`.

## In practice

Every ND statement on `map.mathshell.org`, as fetched 2026-08-08. No two of them use the same
words.

| Surface | Verbatim phrasing | Coverage recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage "Free to Schools" sidebox | "may be reproduced as-is for non-commercial use. Precise terms vary between materials." | Site-wide, and it declines a site-wide grant in the same breath |
| Classroom Challenges, per-lesson sidebox | "may be copied and distributed, unmodified" | Byte-identical on all 5 lesson pages checked |
| Summative Assessment Tasks, per-task sidebox | "may be copied and distributed, unmodified" | Byte-identical across all 4 task pages checked |
| Prototype Tests page | "may be distributed, unmodified" | Drops "copied"; same licence URL |
| Classroom Challenge lesson PDF, cover | "May be reproduced, unmodified, for non-commercial purposes" | Plus a differently worded final page |
| Same PDF, final page | "This material may be reproduced and distributed, without modification, for non-commercial purposes" | Third phrasing of one rider |
| Teacher guide PDF | The rider stated twice, cover and front matter | Unlike the summative task PDFs |

Every one that names the instrument links `http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0/`,
plain `http://`, with no jurisdiction code, so the instrument is 3.0 **Unported**. "All other
rights reserved" is appended to every ND grant on that host.

**The publisher's own headline is that there is no single answer.** Verbatim: "Precise terms vary
between materials." That is why a reader must resolve the collection before the verdict, and the
PD Modules are the live counterexample: ShareAlike, no "unmodified", no "all other rights
reserved".

**The file and the page that serves it disagree.** `hopewell_geometry.pdf` carries, as a running
footer on both pages, verbatim: "Copyright © 2011 by Mathematics Assessment Resource Service. All
rights reserved." There is no Creative Commons text in the file. The CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 grant for it
exists only on the HTML page linking to it. Its rubric PDF, 144,913 bytes, extracted cleanly and
returned zero hits for copyright, licence, creative commons or rights reserved. **Anyone who
receives the PDF alone sees pure all-rights-reserved.** Cite the page and archive the page, not
the file. See [[license-all-rights-reserved]] and [[license-unmarked-silence]].

**ND also appears outside Creative Commons.** The `learnwithsap.org` e² Instructional Practice
Suite terms bar, verbatim in part, `Modify, adapt, translate, or create derivative works from the
e² Tools without SAP's prior written permission`, alongside a public-redistribution bar naming
`websites, social media, marketplaces, or AI training datasets`. None of the Creative Commons
machinery on this page interprets that document. See [[source-achieve-the-core-sap]].

**No licence page exists on the ND host.** Fourteen probed paths returned 404 on
`map.mathshell.org`, including `/terms`, `/copyright`, `/permissions`, `/license`, `/licence`,
`/about`, `/faq` and `/legal`. The About page is `/background.php` and carries no terms text. The
grant appears only on individual resource pages, never on the browse indexes. See
[[trap-license-lives-off-the-obvious-page]].

## Gotchas & constraints

**1. NC is not the strict one here.** ND blocks the operation a curriculum repo actually performs.
NonCommercial would be dormant while nothing is sold; ND is binding immediately and permanently. A
reader who checks only the NC term on this host reaches the wrong verdict. See
[[license-noncommercial]].

**2. Do not read the deed's private-modification implication as a permission.** The licence never
grants the right to produce an adaptation and reserves all rights not expressly granted. This wiki
takes the licence.

**3. Citing is completely unaffected.** Naming a MARS task, linking it, stating which standard it
addresses and describing in your own words what it does needs no licence at all. So does writing
original expression of the underlying mathematics, because mathematics is not copyrightable, a
reading recorded by this project's own MARS report. Curate-and-cite is fully clear under ND. See
[[concept-curate-and-cite]] and [[practice-cite-without-redistributing]].

**4. This project's own adjudication is internally inconsistent about quoting under ND, and this
page does not resolve it.** The twelve-host verdict table states in its QUOTING section that
quotation does not trigger NoDerivatives "since the quoted fragment is unmodified", and then lists
the MARS row under "Do not quote", giving ND as the reason. The same document says short attributed
quotation from that row would rest on fair use, "which is a legal judgment no agent verified and
which the document explicitly does not make". The operative posture recorded for the host is
cite-only. **What would close it: a ruling, not a fetch.**

**5. "MARS is ND" is wrong at the host level.** The PD Modules are BY-NC-SA and the TRU Math Suite
carries no statement at all. A single host verdict applied to a URL from the wrong collection is
the specific error the publisher's own "Precise terms vary between materials" sentence warns about.

**6. Format conversion is not a derivative, but the licence's version of that is narrower than the
deed's.** The deed says merely changing the format never creates a derivative; the legal code
grants only modifications `technically necessary` for a media or format shift. Re-typesetting a
task into a lesson package is not obviously either.

**7. The "no 30-day cure period" claim is this project's reading, not a staged quotation.** The
MARS report records that 3.0 Unported has a weaker attribution and DRM regime than 4.0 and no
30-day cure period for violations. **The staged 3.0 deed and legal-code extracts do not contain
that text, so it is unverified from `sources/`.** What would close it: fetch and stage CC BY 4.0
Section 6(b) and compare it against the 3.0 termination clause.

**8. 3.0 Unported is not 3.0 US, and neither ported variant is staged here.** Do not answer a
ported-jurisdiction question from this page.

**9. The upgrade banner is not a withdrawal.** The 3.0 deed recommends 4.0 to licensors choosing a
licence for their own work. Material already licensed under 3.0 stays there, and relabelling a
MARS grant CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 would misstate the attribution duties in both directions.

**10. The creativecommons.org deed page documenting BY-NC-ND 3.0 is itself published under CC BY
4.0.** Its site footer is not evidence about the instrument it documents.

**11. Frozen content is not a frozen grant.** MARS content is static, its footer copyright range
ends 2015 and its newest PDF stamps are © 2015, but nothing here is dated later than 2026-08-08.
See [[license-withdrawn-grants]].
