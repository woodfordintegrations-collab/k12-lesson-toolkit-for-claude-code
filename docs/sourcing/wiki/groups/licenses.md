# license

Eight pages about the **instruments**, not about the hosts that carry them. A `source` page tells
you that `map.mathshell.org` is `cite_only`; this family tells you what NoDerivatives actually
does, why the deed and the legal code disagree about it, and why NonCommercial was not the term
that produced that verdict.

You want this family when you have just read a notice and need to know what it obliges, when you
are choosing between two candidate sources on licence grounds, or when you are about to write a
version number, a credit line or a verdict token into something durable.

Three of the eight rows are riders rather than licences. A rider modifies a CC BY baseline and
does one specific thing to it, and the three behave differently enough that the usual shorthand
gets them wrong in opposite directions: NC has no copyleft, SA has copyleft and no commercial bar
of its own, ND forbids the operation outright and no relicensing cures it.

Four rows are not about a rider at all. They are about what state a source's rights are **in**:
nothing said, something reserved, something dedicated, or something that used to be granted and
is not any more. Silence and asserted reservation resolve to the same destination and are reached
by different evidence, which is why they are two pages.

**No page here settles what a host serves today.** That is a dated fetch against the host with the
sentence pasted, and it lives in [[sources]] under this wiki's evidence floor.

## Pick a page

**Ordering: the baseline first, then the three riders in the order they bite in this corpus, then
the four rows that describe a rights state rather than a rider.**

| Page | What error it prevents |
|---|---|
| [[license-cc-by]] | Silently upgrading a 3.0 Unported label to 4.0. They are separate instruments at separate URLs and the credit line they demand is not the same. |
| [[license-sharealike]] | Believing a reworded task escapes SA. Structure, numbers and the pedagogical move are the test, and a contaminated file is byte-indistinguishable from a clean one. |
| [[license-noncommercial]] | Writing "NC forbids adaptation". It does not, it has no copyleft, and it is dormant rather than absent until something is sold. |
| [[license-noderivatives]] | Blaming NonCommercial for a cite-only verdict that ND caused, and reading the deed's "modify privately" as the licence's position when the 3.0 legal code grants no adaptation right at all. |
| [[license-unmarked-silence]] | Reading no notice as no owner. Silence resolves to all rights reserved, and it was the modal case: 4 of 7 PDFs opened. |
| [[license-all-rights-reserved]] | Writing a verdict off the string. "All **other** rights reserved" inside a live CC grant is one word away and means the opposite. |
| [[license-withdrawn-grants]] | Recording a licence as a property of a source instead of a property of a source at a date. Two grants here vanished inside six months. |
| [[license-public-domain-dedication]] | Writing "CC0" when no host in this corpus ever wrote it, and writing that CC0 "requires attribution" when it imposes nothing at all. |
