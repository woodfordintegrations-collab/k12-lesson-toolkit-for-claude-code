## 2. VERDICT KEY (the document's §2)

**ADJUDICATION.** Three distinct operations. They are not degrees of the same thing, and the
licenses constrain them very differently. Most license anxiety in OER work comes from collapsing
them.

### CITING: always permitted, by every source in this table, without exception

Naming a source, linking to it, stating what standard it addresses, describing in your own words
what it does, and recording the bibliographic facts (title, author, URL, date accessed).

**No copyright license is required to cite.** Facts, titles, URLs and standards alignments are not
protected expression. ShareAlike, NonCommercial and NoDerivatives **do not touch citation**. This
is why the ARR host (openmiddle.com), the ND host (map.mathshell.org), the withdrawn-grant host
(achievethecore.org) and the silent research PDFs (ERIC) all remain fully usable in a
curate-and-cite unit. `EJ1064122` even says so explicitly, VERBATIM (artifact): *"Using the
hyperlinks to the article is not considered a violation of copyright."*

Two mechanical constraints, which are not license constraints:
- **map.mathshell.org**: cite the *web page*, not the PDF. The page carries the grant; the file
  says "all rights reserved".
- **mathmistakes.org** and, for now, **engageny.org**: cite a Wayback URL, or the link lands the
  reader on a PHP stack trace or an expired certificate.

### QUOTING: reproducing their exact expression, in quotation marks, with attribution

Pasting a task statement, a lesson narrative sentence, a rubric line, a standard's text.

Permitted where a CC license is in force and its conditions are met. **Quoting does not trigger
ShareAlike**, because a quotation set inside your own original prose with attribution is not an
adaptation of the quoted work, it is a use of it. Nor does it trigger NoDerivatives, since the
quoted fragment is unmodified.

- **Clear to quote:** rows 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 (all carry a live CC grant).
- **Quote but NonCommercial attaches:** rows 4, 5, 6. Irrelevant while nothing is sold, permanent
  if the repo ever monetizes.
- **Do not quote:** rows 7, 9, 10, 11, 12. Either ND (7), unlicensed or silent (9, 10, 11, 12), or
  a withdrawn grant (11). Short attributed quotation from these would rest on fair use, which is a
  legal judgment no agent verified and which the document explicitly does not make.
- **Never quote regardless of the host grant:** the four AMC-derived IM tasks (1002, 1009, 916,
  918). IM's grant cannot convey rights IM does not hold.

### PARAPHRASING-AND-REPUBLISHING: rewriting their material into our own words and shipping it

This is the operation the repo actually performs, and it is the one the licenses bite on. A close
paraphrase that follows a specific task's structure, numbers and pedagogical move is an
**adaptation** in copyright terms, however different the wording.

- **NoDerivatives (map.mathshell.org, and the e² Suite) FORBIDS this outright.** VERBATIM (host):
  "Reproduced as-is", "copied and distributed, unmodified", "reproduced and distributed, without
  modification". Stated three separate ways on that host.
- **ShareAlike (tasks.illustrativemathematics.org, EngageNY, ATC's IM-sourced PDFs, LC's 6,214
  Georgia records) PERMITS this but forces the derivative under the same license**, which on those
  hosts means CC BY-NC-SA, dragging a NonCommercial obligation onto whatever file it lands in. Keep
  SA-derived material in quarantined files, or write from the standard instead.
- **CC BY 4.0 / CC BY 3.0 / CC BY-NC 4.0 (rows 1, 2, 3, 4, 8) PERMIT this cleanly.** Adaptation is
  expressly allowed, no copyleft. Attribution, license hyperlink, and an **indication that changes
  were made** must travel with it.

**The safe default the document recommends:** write original expression from the **standard text**
(row 3, CC BY 4.0), cite everyone, quote only from rows 1/2/3/4/5/6/8, and adapt only from rows
1/2/3/8, plus row 4 while nothing is sold. That path carries no copyleft into the repo.

### Verdict values used in the table

| Value | Cite | Quote | Paraphrase-and-republish |
|---|---|---|---|
| **QUOTE + ADAPT** | yes | yes | yes, no copyleft |
| **QUOTE + ADAPT (NonCommercial)** | yes | yes | yes, but NC attaches downstream |
