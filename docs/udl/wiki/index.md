# Universal Design for Learning

A source-grounded wiki on UDL, built against **CAST's UDL Guidelines 3.0** (released
30 July 2024). 73 pages.

**New here?** Go to [[when-to-use]]. It routes by the problem you actually have.

**One thing to know first.** Version 3.0 renamed a lot. *Checkpoints* are now
**considerations**, *provide options* is now **design options**, and the framework's
goal moved from *expert learners* to **[[learner-agency]]**. Five of the nine
guidelines changed name. Most UDL material online still describes version 2.2, so
resource-to-resource vocabulary mismatches are expected. [[taxonomy]] has the
full mapping.

## How this wiki was built

Every page is grounded in staged sources under `sources/`: CAST's own pages for the
framework, peer-reviewed literature for the evidence layer, primary legal sources for
statutory claims. Nothing is written from model memory, which matters more here than
in most domains because the framework changed under a version boundary that most
training data predates.

Where a source could not be confirmed, the page says so rather than asserting.
Where CAST asserts a design move without evidence, the page says that too.

Build record, gate rulings, and known caveats: `meta/`. Scope and what was
deliberately excluded: `INVENTORY.md`.
