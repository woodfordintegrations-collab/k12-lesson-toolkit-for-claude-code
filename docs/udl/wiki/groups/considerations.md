# Considerations

The framework's third level: 36 considerations, each sitting under one guideline.
These are where UDL stops being a stance and starts being a set of moves.

**Vocabulary note.** These were called *checkpoints* in version 2.2. If you are
working from older material or a district binder, "checkpoint 7.1" and "consideration
7.1" are the same node. CAST also renamed the fourth level: the strategies inside each
consideration page are now *design options*, not "provide options".

## Pairs that are constantly confused

These are the ones to check before you cite a number at someone.

| These two | The difference |
|---|---|
| 2.5 [[illustrate-through-multiple-media]] vs 5.1 [[multiple-media-for-communication]] | 2.5 is the *material* being presented through media. 5.1 is the *learner* expressing through media. Representation vs Action & Expression. |
| 6.1 [[set-meaningful-goals]] vs 8.1 [[meaning-and-purpose-of-goals]] | 6.1 is the learner setting goals (Action & Expression). 8.1 is making a goal's purpose clear so effort is sustained (Engagement). |
| 1.2 [[multiple-ways-to-perceive-information]] vs 3.3 [[multiple-ways-of-knowing]] | 1.2 is modality: can the information be taken in? 3.3 is epistemology: whose knowledge counts as knowledge? |
| 5.3 [[build-fluencies-with-graduated-support]] vs 7.1 [[optimize-choice-and-autonomy]] | 5.3 fades support. 7.1 offers control. Different moves, often mistaken for each other. |
| 9.2 [[awareness-of-self-and-others]] vs 9.3 [[individual-and-collective-reflection]] | 9.2 builds the capacity to notice. 9.3 creates the occasion to look back. |
