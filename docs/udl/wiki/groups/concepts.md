# Concepts

The cross-cutting ideas the framework runs on. None of them is a guideline, and all of
them are referenced by many. If you read only three pages in this wiki, read
[[firm-goals-flexible-means]], [[construct-irrelevant-variance]], and
[[learner-variability]].

## The operating core

| Concept | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [[firm-goals-flexible-means]] | The central move: separate the construct to be learned from the means of reaching or showing it. Every other decision depends on getting this right first. |
| [[construct-irrelevant-variance]] | The measurement-theory version of the same idea, and the strongest bridge between UDL and psychometrics. Also carries the accommodation vs modification distinction. |
| [[learner-variability]] | The premise the framework rests on: variability is systematic, not noise around an average. |
| [[barriers-and-learner-environment-mismatch]] | CAST's position that the curriculum, not the learner, is what is disabled. |

## Doctrine versus folk-UDL

A large amount of what circulates as UDL appears in no CAST document. The concept
pages label these where they arise. "Jagged learning profile", "designing for the
edges", and "plus-one" are all practitioner coinages rather than CAST doctrine. They
are useful ideas and they are not the framework, and a wiki that blurred the two would
be less useful than one that does not.
