# Guidelines

The nine guidelines, each sitting at one intersection of the framework's two axes:
a **principle** (column) and a **row** (Access, Support, or Executive Function).

Reading down a column asks "what can I vary about engagement?". Reading across a row
asks "what does getting in the door require, whatever the principle?". Both are real
questions and the grid is the only view that supports both. See
[[access-support-executive-function]].

| Row | What the row is asking | Engagement | Representation | Action & Expression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Can they get in the door at all? | [[welcoming-interests-and-identities]] (7) | [[perception]] (1) | [[interaction]] (4) |
| **Support** | Once in, what keeps them going? | [[sustaining-effort-and-persistence]] (8) | [[language-and-symbols]] (2) | [[expression-and-communication]] (5) |
| **Executive Function** | Can they direct the work themselves? | [[emotional-capacity]] (9) | [[building-knowledge]] (3) | [[strategy-development]] (6) |

The row question is the same across all three columns; only the medium changes. A
learner locked out at the Access row is locked out whether the lock is a screen they
cannot read (1), a device they cannot operate (4), or a task they see no reason to
begin (7). That equivalence is the argument for the grid, and it is invisible if you
read the framework as three separate lists of three.

**Row-first is often the faster diagnosis.** If a learner is not engaging at all,
check the whole Access row before working down any single column.

## Renamed in 3.0

Five of the nine changed name in the 2024 revision. If you trained on UDL before then,
these are the ones you will search for and not find.

| 2.2 name | 3.0 name |
|---|---|
| Comprehension | [[building-knowledge]] (3) |
| Physical Action | [[interaction]] (4) |
| Executive Functions | [[strategy-development]] (6) |
| Recruiting Interest | [[welcoming-interests-and-identities]] (7) |
| Self-Regulation | [[emotional-capacity]] (9) |

Unchanged: [[perception]] (1), [[language-and-symbols]] (2),
[[expression-and-communication]] (5), [[sustaining-effort-and-persistence]] (8).

Two of these renames are substantive rather than cosmetic. Guideline 7 now centres
intersecting identities, and Guideline 9 reframes from a trait the learner regulates
into a property of the environment you design.

## A naming collision worth knowing

Guideline 6 is called **Strategy Development**, and the row it sits in is called
**Executive Function**. In version 2.2, Guideline 6 was itself named "Executive
Functions". So the row and the node share a history and are routinely conflated. The
row spans Guidelines 3, 6, and 9. The guideline is one node inside it.
