# Evidence

Five appraisals of specific claims made about UDL. Each states a claim precisely
enough to be falsifiable, then reports what the research does and does not support,
with numbers quoted rather than characterized.

This layer exists because UDL is unusually prone to being oversold, including by
federal statute. A wiki that only described the framework would leave a reader unable
to tell which parts are demonstrated and which are asserted.

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| [[does-udl-improve-student-outcomes]] | A moderate pooled effect exists. What it is an effect *of* is genuinely unsettled, because the studies do not define UDL the same way. |
| [[measuring-udl-implementation-fidelity]] | Instruments exist and are reliable. Reliable is not valid, and this question sits upstream of every other one here. |
| [[udl-and-the-learning-styles-problem]] | UDL is not the meshing hypothesis. That acquittal is narrower than it looks, and does not supply the missing outcome evidence. |
| [[the-neuroscience-basis-claim]] | The warrant is thin. A thin warrant does not make the design advice wrong, and separating those two is the useful move. |
| [[scientifically-valid-the-statutory-claim]] | Federal law calls UDL "a scientifically valid framework". That is a 2008 legislative stipulation, not a finding. |

## Read them in this order

If you want the honest picture rather than a single verdict, start with
[[measuring-udl-implementation-fidelity]]. If "this classroom implements UDL" is not a
determinate fact, no outcome study can be interpreted, because the independent
variable is undefined. Everything else in this layer inherits that problem.

Then [[does-udl-improve-student-outcomes]] for what the numbers actually are, then the
two claim-specific pages, then the statutory one for how the framework's status gets
misdescribed in citation.

## The stance

None of these pages is an argument against UDL, and none should be read as one. Much
of what UDL recommends is ordinary good practice: clear goals, accessible materials,
scaffolding that fades, feedback that tells you what to do next. The framework's
vulnerability is not in the practices. It is in the empirical claims made on its
behalf, and in the gap between those claims and the literature that is supposed to
support them.

Being useful here means being the source that knows where the framework is strong and
where it is merely asserted.
