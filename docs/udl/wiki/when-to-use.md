# When to Use This Wiki

A routing surface. Start here when you know your problem but not which page holds the
answer.

## First: is UDL even the right frame?

| Your actual situation | Where to go |
|---|---|
| A student has an IEP or 504 plan and you are deciding what you owe them | **Not UDL.** That is a legal obligation. [[assuming-udl-replaces-accommodations]] explains why, then talk to whoever owns compliance. |
| You need to know whether a digital material is legally accessible | **Not UDL.** That is WCAG and a testable standard. [[mistaking-udl-for-accessibility-compliance]]. |
| A specific student is struggling and you want to respond to them | That is differentiated instruction, which is fine and is not this. [[confusing-udl-with-differentiated-instruction]] |
| You are designing something before you know who is in the room | **Yes. This is UDL.** Start at [[design-a-lesson-with-udl]]. |
| Someone told you to "do UDL" and you want to know if it works | [[does-udl-improve-student-outcomes]] |

## Four things this wiki will tell you that CAST will not

1. **The framework was rewritten in July 2024 and most of the internet has not
   noticed.** Checkpoints are now considerations, "expert learners" is now learner
   agency, and five of nine guidelines were renamed. If a resource still says
   "checkpoint 7.1", it predates the current framework. See [[learner-agency]] and
   [[access-support-executive-function]].
2. **The evidence is thinner than the advocacy.** The best pooled estimate is moderate
   and rests on studies that do not agree on what UDL is. The federal statute's
   "scientifically valid" is a definition, not a finding.
3. **Much of what circulates as UDL is not CAST doctrine.** "Jagged learning profile",
   "designing for the edges", "plus-one": all practitioner coinages. Useful, but not
   the framework.
4. **UDL cannot discharge a legal obligation.** It has no success criteria and no
   conformance test. This is the wiki's highest-stakes point and it has two pages:
   [[assuming-udl-replaces-accommodations]] and
   [[mistaking-udl-for-accessibility-compliance]].
