# 8.4 Foster Belonging and Community

## Summary

Consideration 8.4 sits under [[sustaining-effort-and-persistence]] (Guideline 8) in
[[multiple-means-of-engagement]]. Its brief from CAST is "Cultivate spaces where
learners are wanted and where learners want to be." CAST names this "particularly
vital for historically excluded and marginalized learners."

## When to reach for it

Reach for 8.4 when the block is a learner's sense of legitimacy or safety in the room
itself: independent of any particular collaborative task or activity structure.

| The actual problem | Where to go instead |
|---|---|
| The lever you have available is a collaborative task structure | [[collaboration-and-collective-learning]] (8.3) |
| Active bias, threat, or distraction is present in the environment | [[biases-threats-and-distractions]] (7.4) |
| The issue is representation within instructional content itself | [[diversity-of-perspectives-and-identities]] (1.3) |
| The issue sits upstream, at the initial welcome of interests and identities | [[welcoming-interests-and-identities]] (Guideline 7) |

Notably, CAST's own fourth design option for 8.4 is "Welcome interests and identities
(referencing Guideline 7)": 8.4 explicitly points back to the guideline that
[[welcoming-interests-and-identities]] heads, rather than restating that ground
itself. It's the one place in this set of five pages where a CAST design option is a
pointer to another guideline rather than a self-contained move.

**8.4 is not [[collaboration-and-collective-learning]] (8.3).** 8.3 is a method for
organizing work together; 8.4 is whether a learner feels they belong in the room at
all, whether or not any collaborative task is happening. Running collaborative
structures well does not, by itself, produce belonging, and a learner can feel
belonging in a room that rarely asks them to work with others.

## How it works

CAST's description:

> "To support learners to sustain effort and persistence — and to thrive as learners
> more broadly — it is critical to design learning environments where learners feel
> a legitimate sense of belonging and community. This is particularly vital for
> historically excluded and marginalized learners. Sources and meanings of belonging
> and community will vary across learners and contexts."

Two bounds are worth naming directly. CAST flags this as *particularly* vital for
"historically excluded and marginalized learners" without further defining which
groups that phrase covers: this page reports that qualifier as CAST's own wording
rather than expanding it with examples the source doesn't give. And CAST explicitly
declines to define belonging as one fixed thing: "sources and meanings... will vary
across learners and contexts."

## In practice

CAST's design options for 8.4:

- Create opportunities for learners to share their perspectives on what belonging and
  community can feel like and look like.
- Create opportunities for learners to share their ideas for different ways to foster
  belonging and community.
- Welcome interests and identities (referencing Guideline 7).
- Examine when or how bias may be creating barriers to learners' sense of belonging.

**A worked pass.** At the start of a term, rather than declaring what belonging looks
like, ask learners to define it themselves: open-ended, discussion or written
(first and second options). Treat [[welcoming-interests-and-identities]] as already
in force rather than re-deriving it (third option). Periodically examine concrete
classroom practices (who gets called on, whose names are consistently
mispronounced, whose examples appear in materials) for bias creating barriers to
belonging specifically, not barriers to access generally (fourth option).

## Gotchas & constraints

- **This page has genuinely less operational detail than its neighbors in this
  guideline.** Four design options, against five for [[meaning-and-purpose-of-goals]]
  (8.1), eight for [[collaboration-and-collective-learning]] (8.3), and six for
  [[action-oriented-feedback]] (8.5). That thinness is a real feature of the source,
  reported as-is rather than padded to match the others.
- **"Historically excluded and marginalized learners" is CAST's own phrase**,
  presented here as CAST's qualifier and not expanded with named groups the source
  doesn't specify.
- **The 8.3/8.4 blur runs both directions.** See
  [[collaboration-and-collective-learning]] for the same distinction stated from the
  collaboration side.
- **Research not retrieved.** The source page references a CAST research section
  without detailing findings. See [[does-udl-improve-student-outcomes]] for the
  evidence picture at the framework level.
