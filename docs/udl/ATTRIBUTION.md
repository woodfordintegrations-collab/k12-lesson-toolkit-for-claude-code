# Attribution and rights — UDL reference wiki

This wiki is a reference on Universal Design for Learning. It rests substantially on CAST's
UDL Guidelines, and CAST's rights position is narrower than people generally assume, so it
is stated here plainly rather than left to inference.

## CAST's position, as verified

**The UDL Guidelines carry no Creative Commons licence.**

Verified at <https://udlguidelines.cast.org> on 2026-08-11. The site states:

> © CAST, Inc. 2026

and gives a suggested citation:

> CAST (2024). CAST Universal Design for Learning Guidelines version 3.0.
> Retrieved from https://udlguidelines.cast.org

There is no CC BY, no CC BY-NC, no CC BY-NC-ND, and no open-licence statement of any kind on
the Guidelines site. **Absence of a licence is not permission.** Under Berne, unmarked
material is all rights reserved.

A caution worth recording, because it is the likely source of the widespread belief that the
Guidelines are openly licensed: *other* CAST publications do carry Creative Commons terms —
the UDL Schoolwide Implementation Criteria guide, for instance, is CC BY-NC-ND. That is a
different document. A licence on one publication by an organisation says nothing about
another, and this is precisely the per-resource trap the sourcing wiki documents for
Achieve the Core, MARS and ERIC.

## What that permits, and what this wiki therefore does

Three operations, kept apart:

| Operation | Permitted here | What this wiki does |
|---|---|---|
| **Cite** | yes, no licence needed | every page lists the exact CAST URLs it draws on, in its frontmatter |
| **Quote** | short, attributed, for commentary | used sparingly and marked |
| **Paraphrase and republish** | **no** | not done |

These pages are **original commentary written about the Guidelines**, not a restatement of
them. A reader who wants CAST's own wording must go to CAST, and every page carries the link
that takes them there. The guideline numbering and titles (`9.4 Cultivate Empathy and
Restorative Practices`, and so on) are CAST's naming, reproduced as short factual labels so
that a reader can find the corresponding page on CAST's site.

**If you want the UDL Guidelines themselves, go to <https://udlguidelines.cast.org>.** This
wiki is not a substitute for them and does not attempt to be one.

## Required citation

Any work that draws on the Guidelines through this wiki should cite CAST directly, not this
wiki:

> CAST (2024). CAST Universal Design for Learning Guidelines version 3.0.
> Retrieved from https://udlguidelines.cast.org
