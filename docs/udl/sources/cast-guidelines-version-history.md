# CAST UDL Guidelines Version History

## Version Lineage with Dates

| Version | Release Date | Key Characteristics |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1.0 | 2008 | Original framework foundation |
| 2.0 | 2011 | Expanded full text documentation |
| 2.1 | 2014 | Shifted Engagement to first principle; reorganized horizontal rows |
| 2.2 | 2018 | Reordered rows based on practitioner feedback; Access row moved to top; launched with comprehensive website |
| 3.0 | July 30, 2024 | Most extensive update; four-year community-driven research process |

## Version 2.2 Structure (Prior Major Release)

### Three Principles
1. **Engagement** (the "why" of learning)
   - Recruiting Interest
   - Sustaining Effort & Persistence
   - Self-Regulation

2. **Representation** (the "what" of learning)
   - Perception
   - Language & Symbols
   - Comprehension

3. **Action & Expression** (the "how" of learning)
   - Physical Action
   - Expression & Communication
   - Executive Function

### Horizontal Organization
Guidelines organized vertically into three horizontal rows:
- Access
- Build
- Internalize

### Official Goal Statement (2.2)
To develop "expert learners" who are "resourceful and knowledgeable, strategic and goal-directed, purposeful and motivated." (Source: [CAST search results on expert learners terminology](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/))

---

## Version 3.0 Structure (Current Release)

### Three Principles
1. **Design Multiple Means of Engagement**
   - Access (Welcoming Interests & Identities)
   - Support (Sustaining Effort & Persistence)
   - Executive Function (Emotional Capacity)

2. **Design Multiple Means of Representation**
   - Perception
   - Language & Symbols
   - Building Knowledge

3. **Design Multiple Means of Action & Expression**
   - Interaction
   - Expression & Communication
   - Strategy Development

### Official Goal Statement (3.0)

CAST states verbatim:

> "The goal of UDL is **learner agency** that is purposeful & reflective, resourceful & authentic, strategic & action-oriented."

Full definition from [CAST's UDL Goal page](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/more/udl-goal/):

> "learner agency, the capacity to actively participate in making choices in service of learning goals"

## Detailed Changes from 2.2 to 3.0

### Terminology & Language Mapping

| 2.2 Term | 3.0 Term | Category |
|----------|----------|----------|
| "Provide Options" | "Design Options" | Framework language |
| "Checkpoints" | "Considerations" | Guidance structure |
| Recruiting Interest | Welcoming Interests and Identities | Guideline |
| Self-Regulation | Emotional Capacity | Guideline |
| Comprehension | Building Knowledge | Guideline |
| Physical Action | Interaction | Guideline |
| Executive Functions | Strategy Development | Guideline |
| Rows: Access/Build/Internalize | Rows: Access/Support/Executive Function | Organization |

**Language Philosophy Shift:** Removed "provide" language throughout all principles and guidelines. Per [Novak Education analysis](https://www.novakeducation.com/blog/what-to-know-about-the-udl-guidelines-3.0-update), this shift "signals the guidelines should function as a collaborative design tool rather than an adult-centric checklist."

### Structural Reorganization

1. **Principle Reframing:** All three principles now explicitly use "Design Multiple Means of" language, emphasizing co-design rather than educator provision.

2. **Executive Function Restructuring:** Executive function was "integrated across multiple guidelines instead of standing alone, better reflecting neuroscience research." (Source: [Novak Education](https://www.novakeducation.com/blog/what-to-know-about-the-udl-guidelines-3.0-update))

3. **Horizontal Row Labels:** Changed from Access/Build/Internalize to Access/Support/Executive Function, with specific sub-labels (e.g., Access now explicitly includes "Welcoming Interests & Identities").

## The "Expert Learners" → "Learner Agency" Shift

### What Happened to "Expert Learners"

The phrase "expert learners" was entirely removed and replaced with "learner agency" as the overarching goal.

### CAST's Stated Rationale (Verbatim)

According to CAST, stakeholders during the update process identified concerns with "expert learners":

> "The term 'expert' implies ideas of exclusivity and a finite ending to the learning process and devalues the importance of collectively generating knowledge."

Further, stakeholders emphasized:

> "The conceptualization of 'expert learning' devalues the importance of collectively generating knowledge and fails to recognize the brilliance inherent in every learner."

### Why "Learner Agency" Instead

The new goal emphasizes:
- Learners learning **together** and sharing experiences
- Recognizing the **brilliance inherent in every learner** rather than implying assimilation
- Capacity to **actively participate in making choices** in service of learning goals
- Regulating affective, cognitive, and behavioral processes
- Reducing the connotation of learning as finite
- Intentional environmental design that changes the environment rather than framing learners as having deficits

Source: [CAST's UDL Goal page](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/more/udl-goal/) states:

> "UDL aims to change the design of the environment rather than to situate the problem as a perceived deficit within the learner."

## Broader Rationale for Version 3.0 Update

### Official CAST Statement on Why 3.0 Was Needed

According to the [official update announcement](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/more/about-guidelines-3-0/), the revision:

> "Emphasizes addressing barriers rooted in biases and systems of exclusion" and aims to "reduce barriers and more fully honor and value every learner."

### Three Core Shifts in 3.0

1. **Identity as Variability:** Emphasizing learners' **multiple and intersecting identities** as part of variability, moving beyond ability-based considerations alone.

2. **Asset-Based Framing:** Putting UDL in conversation with other **asset-based approaches and theoretical frameworks**, including pedagogies that center, value, and sustain learners' cultural practices.

3. **Language Shift:** Moving from **educator-centered to learner-centered language**, with Guidelines 3.0 intentionally using verbs that can be used interchangeably among and between educators and learners.

### Research Process

The 3.0 update involved:
- Four-year, community-driven research process
- Over 40 focus groups conducted
- Researched over 1,100 unique titles
- Extensive literature reviews
- Inclusion of perspectives "previously underrepresented in earlier versions"

Source: [CAST's official process article](https://www.cast.org/resources/tips-articles/udl-guidelines-3-0-a-community-driven-research-based-process-toward-fulfilling-the-promise-of-universal-design-for-learning/)

## Availability of Prior Versions

### Status of Prior Version Web Pages

The prior versions (1.0–2.2) **do not have separate live web pages**. Instead:

- **All versions available via Downloads page:** The [CAST UDL Guidelines Downloads page](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/more/downloads/) offers downloadable PDF and graphic organizer files for all versions dating back to 2008.

- **Version 2.2 (2018):** Released alongside the launch of CAST's comprehensive UDL Guidelines website; PDFs remain accessible at direct URLs such as:
  - https://udlguidelines.cast.org/static/udlg2.2-text-a11y.pdf (accessibility-optimized text)
  - Graphic organizers in numbered and non-numbered formats

- **Version 2.1 (2014) and earlier:** Available primarily as downloadable graphic organizers; no dedicated web pages.

- **Implications for third-party materials:** Resources citing version 2.2 structure will find the original framework intact as static PDFs, but the main website (udlguidelines.cast.org) now displays 3.0 content only.

## Summary: Key Shifts at a Glance

| Dimension | Version 2.2 | Version 3.0 |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Goal** | Develop "expert learners" | Cultivate "learner agency" |
| **Framework Language** | "Provide Options" | "Design Options" |
| **Guidance Unit Name** | Checkpoints | Considerations |
| **Core Innovation** | Accessibility-focused | Identity and bias-aware; asset-based |
| **Learner Framing** | Deficit-reduction | Brilliance recognition + agency |
| **Identity Treatment** | Not primary | Central to variability |
| **Word Choices** | Educator-centered verbs ("provide") | Learner-centered, interchangeable verbs |
| **Row Organization** | Access/Build/Internalize | Access/Support/Executive Function |
