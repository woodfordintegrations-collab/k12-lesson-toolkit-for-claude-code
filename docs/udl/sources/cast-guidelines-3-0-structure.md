# CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0 Complete Structure Map

## Version Information
- **Current Version:** 3.0
- **Release Date:** July 30, 2024
- **Source:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/

## Hierarchy Vocabulary
The CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0 uses a four-level hierarchy:
1. **Principles** (3 total)
2. **Guidelines** (9 total)
3. **Considerations** (36 total)
4. **Design Options** (strategies/recommendations within each consideration)

## Learner Outcome Goal
"The goal of UDL is learner agency that is purposeful & reflective, resourceful & authentic, strategic & action-oriented."

---

## PRINCIPLE 1: Design Multiple Means of Engagement

**Official Description (CAST):** "Affect represents a crucial element to the learning process, and learners differ markedly in what sparks their motivation for learning." The framework recognizes learner variability and the importance of learners' "multiple and intersecting identities" in the educational process. "There is not one means of engagement that will be optimal for all learners in all contexts; multiple options for engagement are essential."

**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/

### Guideline 7: Welcoming Interests & Identities
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/interests-identities/

**Official Description:** "Learners' unique interests as well as their unique identities, including intersecting dimensions such as race/ethnicity, culture, gender, language, and disability, must be recognized, sustained, and valued."

**Considerations:**
- **7.1: Optimize choice and autonomy** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/interests-identities/choice-autonomy/)
  - "Develop agency in the learning process."
  
- **7.2: Optimize relevance, value, and authenticity** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/interests-identities/relevance-value-authenticity/)
  - "Connect learning to experiences that are meaningful and valuable."
  
- **7.3: Nurture joy and play** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/interests-identities/joy-play/)
  - "Spark joy and playfulness in the learning process."
  
- **7.4: Address biases, threats, and distractions** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/interests-identities/biases-threats-distractions/)
  - "Foster spaces to learn and take risks."

### Guideline 8: Sustaining Effort & Persistence
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/effort-persistence/

**Official Description:** "Learning is an ongoing process that oftentimes requires sustained effort and determination." Learners vary significantly in what inspires persistence. Effective designs incorporate meaningful goals, scaffolds for challenging objectives, collaborative environments, and ongoing action-oriented feedback.

**Considerations:**
- **8.1: Clarify the meaning and purpose of goals** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/effort-persistence/meaning-purpose-goals/)
  - Clarify goal meaning and purpose to sustain learner motivation.
  
- **8.2: Optimize challenge and support** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/effort-persistence/challenge-support/)
  - "All learners need to be challenged, but not always in the same way." Learning environments should "embed a variety of tools, resources, and supports to promote successful engagement with the learning goal."
  
- **8.3: Foster collaboration, interdependence, and collective learning** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/effort-persistence/collaboration/)
  - Foster collaboration and community in learning.
  
- **8.4: Foster belonging and community** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/effort-persistence/belonging-community/)
  - Cultivate belonging and community within the learning environment.
  
- **8.5: Offer action-oriented feedback** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/effort-persistence/feedback/)
  - Provide feedback that emphasizes effort and process.

### Guideline 9: Emotional Capacity
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/emotional-capacity/

**Official Description:** Addresses "a critical aspect of human development" by focusing on learners' capabilities to "recognize emotions, manage thoughts and behaviors, and empathize with others." Effective learning environments develop learners' intrinsic abilities to regulate their own emotions and choose how to move forward in service of their learning.

**Considerations:**
- **9.1: Recognize expectations, beliefs, and motivations** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/emotional-capacity/expectations-beliefs-motivations/)
  - Recognize learner expectations, beliefs, and motivations.
  
- **9.2: Develop awareness of self and others** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/emotional-capacity/awareness/)
  - Develop learner awareness of self and others.
  
- **9.3: Promote individual and collective reflection** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/emotional-capacity/reflection/)
  - Promote individual and collective reflection on learning.
  
- **9.4: Cultivate empathy and restorative practices** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/engagement/emotional-capacity/empathy-restorative-practices/)
  - Cultivate empathy and restorative practices in learning communities.

## PRINCIPLE 2: Design Multiple Means of Representation

**Official Description (CAST):** "Learners differ in the ways they perceive and make meaning of information." There is "not one means of representation that will be optimal for every learner"; consequently, "providing options for representation is essential."

**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/

### Guideline 1: Perception
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/perception/

**Official Description:** Ensure "key information is equally perceptible to all learners by: 1) offering the same information through different modalities (e.g., through vision, hearing, or touch); 2) offering information in a format that will allow for adjustability by the user (e.g., text that can be enlarged, sounds that can be amplified); and 3) offering a variety of perspectives."

**Guiding Question:** "How might we increase accessibility, flexibility, and diversity in the sharing of information?"

**Considerations:**
- **1.1: Support opportunities to customize the display of information** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/perception/customize-display/)
  - Customize display settings based on individual needs (font, size, spacing, color, contrast, audio properties, timing).
  
- **1.2: Support multiple ways to perceive information** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/perception/ways-perceive-information/)
  - Provide information through multiple modalities beyond text and images.
  
- **1.3: Represent a diversity of perspectives and identities in authentic ways** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/perception/perspectives-identities/)
  - Incorporate diverse and authentic representations of perspectives and identities.

### Guideline 2: Language & Symbols
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/language-symbols/

**Official Description:** Address how learners vary in their ability to understand different forms of representation. "Inequalities arise when information is presented to all learners through a single form of representation." Ensure that "multiple representations are available not only for accessibility, but for clarity, comprehensibility, and creating a shared understanding for all learners."

**Guiding Question:** "How might we offer, honor, and value multiple representations of language and symbols?"

**Considerations:**
- **2.1: Clarify vocabulary, symbols, and language structures** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/language-symbols/vocabulary-symbols-structure/)
  - Clarify vocabulary, symbols, and language structures for all learners.
  
- **2.2: Support decoding of text, mathematical notation, and symbols** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/language-symbols/text-notation-symbols/)
  - Support decoding of text, math notation, and symbols.
  
- **2.3: Cultivate understanding and respect across languages and dialects** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/language-symbols/languages-dialects/)
  - Cultivate understanding and respect across languages and dialects.
  
- **2.4: Address biases in the use of language and symbols** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/language-symbols/biases/)
  - Address biases present in language and symbol use.
  
- **2.5: Illustrate through multiple media** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/language-symbols/multiple-media/)
  - Illustrate concepts through multiple media formats.

### Guideline 3: Building Knowledge
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/building-knowledge/

**Official Description:** Help learners transform information into usable knowledge. "Building usable knowledge, knowledge that is accessible for future decision-making, depends not upon merely perceiving information, but upon active skills" such as making connections, synthesizing information, and integrating new learning with existing understanding. "Intentional design and multiple representations of information can offer the options and scaffolds necessary to ensure all learners have access to knowledge."

**Considerations:**
- **3.1: Connect prior knowledge to new learning** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/building-knowledge/prior-knowledge/)
  - Connect prior knowledge to new learning experiences.
  
- **3.2: Highlight and explore patterns, critical features, big ideas, and relationships** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/building-knowledge/patterns-features/)
  - Help learners distinguish between critical and unimportant information. "One of the big differences between experts and novices in any domain is the facility with which they distinguish what is critical from what is unimportant or irrelevant."
  
- **3.3: Cultivate multiple ways of knowing and making meaning** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/building-knowledge/making-meaning/)
  - Support multiple epistemologies and ways of constructing knowledge.
  
- **3.4: Maximize transfer and generalization** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/building-knowledge/transfer-generalization/)
  - Help learners transfer learning to new contexts and generalize knowledge.

## PRINCIPLE 3: Design Multiple Means of Action & Expression

**Official Description (CAST):** "Learners differ in the ways they navigate a learning environment, approach the learning process, and express what they know." No single means of action and expression works optimally for all learners, making it essential to provide multiple options.

**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/

### Guideline 4: Interaction
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/interaction/

**Official Description:** "Design materials and physical environments that ensure access and participation for every learner." Traditional formats often restrict how learners can navigate and interact, creating barriers. "Intentionally designed curricular materials provide a seamless interface with common assistive and accessible technologies," enabling all learners, including those with disabilities, to engage through varied interaction methods.

**Considerations:**
- **4.1: Vary and honor the methods for response, navigation, and movement** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/interaction/response-navigation-movement/)
  - Provide flexible response options, adjust rate/timing requirements, support alternative input methods (voice, switches, keyboards), and design flexible physical spaces.
  
- **4.2: Optimize access to accessible materials and assistive and accessible technologies and tools** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/interaction/assistive-technologies/)
  - Ensure access to assistive technologies and accessible tools.

### Guideline 5: Expression & Communication
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/expression-communication/

**Official Description:** "There is no medium of expression that is equally suited for all learners or for all kinds of communication." The objective is to "provide multiple modalities for expression to reduce communication barriers and support learners to express knowledge, ideas, and concepts in the learning environment."

**Guiding Question:** "How might we design learning environments that value multiple and novel modes of expression and communication, including those that have been historically deprioritized?"

**Considerations:**
- **5.1: Use multiple media for communication** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/expression-communication/multiple-media/)
  - Support communication through multiple media.
  
- **5.2: Use multiple tools for construction, composition, and creativity** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/expression-communication/construction-composition-creativity/)
  - Provide multiple tools for creating and composing ideas.
  
- **5.3: Build fluencies with graduated support for practice and performance** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/expression-communication/fluencies-practice-performance/)
  - Build fluency through graduated practice and performance opportunities.
  
- **5.4: Address biases related to modes of expression and communication** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/expression-communication/bias/)
  - Address biases in valuing different modes of expression.

### Guideline 6: Strategy Development
**URL:** https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/

**Official Description:** "Provide scaffolding for executive functions themselves" through two approaches: simplifying lower-level skills to reduce cognitive load, and strengthening higher-level executive skills and strategies. Help learners develop intentional approaches to learning by enabling them to "set long-term goals and plan effective strategies for reaching those goals," including monitoring progress and adjusting tactics.

**Considerations:**
- **6.1: Set meaningful goals** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/goals/)
  - Support goal-setting processes.
  
- **6.2: Anticipate and plan for challenges** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/challenges/)
  - Develop planning skills for addressing anticipated obstacles.
  
- **6.3: Organize information and resources** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/organize-information/)
  - Support organization of information and learning resources.
  
- **6.4: Enhance capacity for monitoring progress** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/monitoring-progress/)
  - Develop self-monitoring and progress-tracking skills.
  
- **6.5: Challenge exclusionary practices** (URL: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/action-expression/strategy-development/exclusionary-practices/)
  - Address and eliminate exclusionary practices in strategy development.

## Node Page Anatomy

Based on fetches of individual principle, guideline, and consideration pages, the standard CAST UDL Guidelines node page contains these sections:

1. **Title and Definition** - The official name and one-line or short official description of the node
2. **Introductory Explanation** - Contextual framing that explains why this principle/guideline/consideration matters
3. **Guiding Question** (for principles and guidelines) - A strategic question framing the design work (e.g., "How might we...")
4. **Core Guidance/Strategies** - For considerations: bulleted list of 3-5 practical design strategies or recommendations. For guidelines: overview of the considerations it contains.
5. **Research Section** - A link to "Research" supporting evidence for the node
6. **Related Nodes Navigation** - Links to adjacent/related considerations within the same guideline or guidelines within the same principle
7. **Site Navigation** - Breadcrumbs, footer with contact information and citation guidance

**Notable:** The sampled pages (1.1, 3.2, 4.1, 8.2) did not include dedicated "reflection questions" or "illustrative examples" sections as distinct subsections. Pages focus on definition + actionable strategies + research links.
